We would like to thank our project supervisor, Mr.\ Suman Shrestha, for his guidance
through the machine learning, data analysis, and web development parts of this project.
His feedback kept the work focused and kept us honest about what the models could and
could not do. We also thank the \gls{docse} for giving us the chance to take on this
project.

Working on credit card fraud detection taught us that the choice of evaluation metric
matters as much as the choice of algorithm. Because fraud is rare, a model can look almost
perfect on accuracy while catching very little real fraud. It also taught us to be careful
about what a single number can support. When we finally put confidence intervals and
significance tests around our results, the apparent gap between our best models turned out
to be well inside the noise. Getting the data split, the resampling, and the decision
threshold right mattered more than any individual model choice.

The five of us learned how to build a small but complete system, from cleaning the data to
training and comparing several models, and then serving the best one behind an API and an
interactive web interface. We are grateful for the resources and support that made this
work possible, and for the time our supervisor spent reviewing our progress.
